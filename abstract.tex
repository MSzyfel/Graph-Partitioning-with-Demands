\begin{abstract}
  We study various graph partitioning tasks subject to general demands. In \textsc{Graph Partitioning with Demands} (GPD), the goal is to delete minimum cost supply edge set so that each remaining component contains at most a prescribed fraction of the total demand. In the hierarchical analogue of this problem, \textsc{Hierarchical Clustering with Demands} (HCD), the goal is to build a hierarchy of clusters, charging each supply edge by the demand in the smallest cluster containing its endpoints.

  Our main general-graph results are approximation algorithms for GPD and HCD. For every fixed $0<\rho<\eta<1$, we give a polynomial-time bi-criteria algorithm for GPD whose components have demand at most $\eta \cdot W$ and cost at most $\mathcal{O}_{\rho,\eta}(\sqrt{\log n})$ times the optimum cost for threshold~$\rho$. This procedure can then be applied recursively to obtain an $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{\log n})$-approximation for HCD. The algorithm for GPD itself iteratively uses a crucial subproblem called \textsc{Generalized Conductance}~(GC), which is a new generalization of the unit-demand \textsc{Sparsest Cut}.

  On supply trees, we show that GC can be solved exactly in polynomial time. We also give bi-criteria algorithms for GPD based on anchored-LP rounding and separator recursion, as well as a single-criterion $(4+\epsilon)$-approximation in $n^{O(1/\rho)}$ time. Moreover, through previous reduction, these GPD algorithms yield a $\frac{4e}{e-1}$-approximation for HCD on trees.
  % Assuming the constant-factor hardness of uniform-demand \textsc{Sparsest Cut} claimed in \cite{OAI:Constant-factor-hardness-of-uniform-sparsest-cut-September-24-2026}, \textsc{Generalized Conductance} is hard to approximate within any constant factor, even with unit demand on every pair of vertices. 

  To complement our algorithmic results, assuming SSEH, we prove that HCD is hard to approximate within any constant factor. This also implies that GPD has no constant-factor bi-criteria approximation, for any fixed $0<\rho<\eta<1$ and GC cannot be approximated within any constant factor as well.

  On trees, assuming the Unique Games Conjecture (UGC), GPD is hard to approximate within a factor of $2-\epsilon$ by reduction from \textsc{Vertex Cover}, while HCD is hard to approximate within a factor of $1.0748$, by reduction from \textsc{Min-Sum Vertex Cover}.
\end{abstract}
